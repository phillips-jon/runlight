# Release Notes for Runlight

## Unreleased

### Added
- The plugin adds Runlight's script to every front-end page and marks 404 pages.
- Control Panel users' own visits can be left out of the count.
- AI agents such as ChatGPT and Claude that fetch your pages are reported to Runlight after the response has been sent.
- A Runlight item in the Control Panel opens your dashboard.
