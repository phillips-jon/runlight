# Web analytics that lives inside your app

Most analytics tools are somewhere else: a script that reports every visit to another company’s servers, a dashboard behind another login, and a bill that grows with your traffic. Runlight is the other way around. It is a package you install in the app that already serves your pages, it keeps its numbers in the database you already run, and it shows them on your own domain.

It counts what you would expect, visitors, pages, sources, places, devices, campaigns, and conversions, and a few things most tools miss: people arriving from ChatGPT and Claude, AI agents reading your pages, and short links on your own domain counted like visits. It does it without cookies and without keeping anything that could say who a visitor was.

Runlight is open source under the MIT license. There is no hosted version and no account to make; the software is the whole product.

Runlight was created and is maintained by [Jon C. Phillips](https://joncphillips.com), a product designer and developer who has been launching web products for over twenty years. Ideas, bug reports, and requests are always welcome: [write any time](/contact/).
